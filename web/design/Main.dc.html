Turn chest X-ray reports into verified findings

Upload one or more radiology reports. RadLabel tags 13 findings, shows the sentence behind every label, checks each one, and answers your questions with citations.

or
Don't paste original reports with personal details. Remove names, dates of birth, record numbers and anything else that identifies a person before uploading. Redaction is your responsibility. Reports are processed in memory, sent to Azure OpenAI for analysis, and never stored.
Analyze reports Try a sample patient (5 reports)

What you get

  1. 13 findings per report: present, uncertain, absent or not mentioned.
  2. Evidence: click a finding to see the sentences behind it.
  3. A check on every label: verified, corrected, suggested or unverified.
  4. Questions about one report, or across all your reports at once, answered with citations.

Measured on 3,086 reports

Macro-F1 0.887 against expert codes. Verified labels were right 94% of the time, unverified ones 39%.

One dataset (Indiana University, ~2013). Labels describe the report text, not the image.

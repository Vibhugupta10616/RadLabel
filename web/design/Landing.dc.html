How it works

A real example, in four steps

One patient, five follow-up X-ray reports. Here is what happens when you upload them.

  1. 01 · Upload
    exam_1.txt exam_2.txt exam_3.pdf exam_4.txt exam_5.txt

    Drop in your reports

    Paste text or upload up to 50 files. Sections and sentences are recognised automatically.

  2. 02 · Label
    Pleural EffusionPresent
    AtelectasisUncertain

    13 findings per report

    A language model reads Findings and Impression and marks each finding present, uncertain, absent or not mentioned.

  3. 03 · Check
    Atelectasispresent→ uncertain
    Corrected: "possibly"

    Every label double-checked

    Rule checks run on everything. Doubtful labels go to a verifier agent that searches the report and decides.

  4. 04 · Ask
    When was the pacemaker placed?
    First seen in report 3 R3·F1

    Ask across all reports

    Your reports are searched for this visit only. Answers cite the exact report and sentence.

Know which labels to trust

Every label carries a badge. In testing, verified labels were right 94% of the time and unverified ones only 39%, so you know exactly where to look twice.

Safe, private and honest by design

What happens to your reports, and what the results do and don't mean.

Full evaluation and limitations on GitHub →
  • Never stored. Reports are processed in memory and forgotten when you leave.
  • You redact. Remove names, dates of birth and record numbers before uploading.
  • Not a medical device. For research and demonstration only.
  • Text, not images. Labels describe what the report says, not the X-ray itself.
  • "Verified" = faithful to the text, not clinically confirmed.
  • Tested on one dataset (Indiana University, ~2013). Other hospitals write differently.
